REVOLUTION CLASSES SEO SETUP

Before deployment, replace EVERY occurrence of:
YOUR-DOMAIN.com
with your real domain, for example:
revolutionclasses.in

Files added:
- robots.txt
- sitemap.xml
- SEO metadata, Open Graph tags and LocalBusiness/EducationalOrganization schema inside index.html

After deployment:
1. Open Google Search Console and add your domain.
2. Verify domain ownership.
3. Submit: https://YOUR-DOMAIN.com/sitemap.xml
4. Request indexing for the homepage.
5. Create/claim Google Business Profile with exactly the same name, address and phone number as the website.
6. Keep the Instagram and website links updated.
7. Icon in Google results: nothing else to do. The homepage links
   favicon.ico + 48/96/144/192px PNG icons (Google needs a square icon
   that is a multiple of 48px). Google picks it up on its next crawl of
   the homepage - usually within a few days of requesting indexing.
   Check with: https://www.google.com/s2/favicons?domain=YOUR-DOMAIN.com&sz=64
8. Share preview (WhatsApp/Facebook): assets/og-image.png (1200x630).

Important: Do not leave YOUR-DOMAIN.com in production. Replace it first.
